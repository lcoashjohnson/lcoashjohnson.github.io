My story

From PR to the newsroom, with a stop at the conference room.

Every role I’ve taken has pointed me back to telling true, useful stories.

I started out interested in public relations. A PR internship with Shawnee Mission School District let me write stories, produce multimedia packages, attend events and learn how to network with adults in the field. It also made me realize that journalism is a great field for me.

Since then I’ve run social media and hosted podcasts, designed a 50-page magazine, and discovered an unexpected love for meeting coordination while keeping multiple conferences moving at once. Each experience taught me to think on my feet, keep an eye on long-term goals, and stay calm when things get busy.

Today I’m studying Journalism and Broadcast Production on a pre-law track. I’m drawn to investigative writing and broadcasting, and I plan to go to law school after I graduate. When I’m not in class, I’m training new teammates at The Mill or serving as Philanthropy and Co-Sisterhood Chair for my sorority.
