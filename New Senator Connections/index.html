How we built this. The 17 senators-elect called by the AP are 11 Democrats (Andrews, Brown, Cooper, El-Sayed, Flanagan, Hamilton, Jackson, Pappas, Stratton, Talarico, Turek) and 6 Republicans (Alme, Barr, Hageman, Hern, Letlow, Moore). Sherrod Brown is returning after a previous Senate term. Races in Alaska and Mississippi were still uncalled, and all results are unofficial until states certify them.
